READING IT
  The sawtooth is the whole point.  A translating pattern must give back the gap
  you imposed and wrap when the gap reaches one period.  Nothing that merely
  weakens attention to the image can produce a non-monotone, periodic recovery.
  `t` moves an offset that is identical for every patch, so it cannot reshape the
  profile at all: it should recover shift 0 at every gap.  `fix` removes the
  p-dependence outright and should also stay at 0.
