READING IT
  The question is which frozen distance leaves the smallest error against ground
  truth on a long answer -- not which one disturbs the model least.  Three things
  can happen.  If every frozen arm sits at the untouched model's long-answer
  error, the intervention does nothing.  If they sit at its short-answer error,
  the fix works and buys back exactly what the drift cost.  If one sits BELOW the
  short-answer error, part of the model's standing spatial bias was the overlay
  all along, and freezing it is worth more than merely removing the drift.
