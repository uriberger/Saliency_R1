whatever it moves is the floor: greedy decoding forks on a single bf16-flipped
token.  An arm that does not clearly beat `null` has not been shown to do
anything at all.

READING IT
  Three outcomes.  If every arm sits on `null`, the intervention does not
  reach generated text and the 0.36 patches E4 measured stay locked inside a
  logit comparison.  If `frozen` beats `null` on divergence but not on NLL, it
  changes the text without improving it.  If it beats `null` on NLL AND the
  gain grows with length, the mechanism reaches the output.
